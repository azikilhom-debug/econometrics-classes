UNCERTAINTYLECTURE 2 · 12/57

Economic relationships are usually not exact mechanical equations

DETERMINISTIC — Y = 2X. Once X is known, Y is fully determined. STOCHASTIC — Y = β₀ + β₁X + u. Even when X is known, Y may differ because of other factors.
Econometric models account for economic uncertainty through a random component.
COMPARISONLECTURE 2 · 13/57

Mathematical model vs econometric model

MATHEMATICAL MODEL — C = a + bY; a theoretical relationship; parameters may remain theoretical symbols; a random component is not required. ECONOMETRIC MODEL — Cᵢ = β₀ + β₁Yᵢ + uᵢ; uses statistical data; parameters are estimated; a random component is present; hypotheses are tested empirically.

An econometric model connects theory with data.

OBJECTIVESLECTURE 2 · 14/57

Three main tasks of an econometric model

01 EXPLANATION — Why are some houses more expensive than others? 02 FORECASTING — How much could sales be next quarter?

03 DECISION AND POLICY ANALYSIS — If the interest rate changes, how might economic indicators respond? The same model is not necessarily equally suitable for all three objectives.

PREDICTION AND EXPLANATIONLECTURE 2 · 15/57

Are prediction and explanation the same task?

PREDICTION — “Which customer is likely to cancel a subscription?” Goal: the most accurate prediction. ECONOMIC EXPLANATION — “Which factors are associated with the probability of cancellation?” Goal: understand the economic nature of the relationship.

High predictive accuracy does not always mean a strong economic explanation.

MODEL CHECKINGLECTURE 2 · 48/57

Stage 7. A regression result is not the end of the research

ECONOMIC LOGIC — Does the coefficient sign agree with theory? STATISTICAL RELIABILITY — Could the result be explained by random variation? MODEL DIAGNOSTICS — How well are the model’s statistical assumptions satisfied?

ROBUSTNESS — Does the result survive other reasonable specifications? An econometric result should not be used for decisions without being checked.

CASE INTERPRETATIONLECTURE 2 · 56/57

How should we state the result?

Illustrative result: β̂₁ = 350.
WRONG: “Delivery increases sales by 350 units.”
BETTER: controlling for the other included factors, sales were on average 350 units higher on days when delivery was available.
Delivery may have been switched on specifically on high-demand days; it may have started together with promotions; seasonal factors may exist.
Correct econometric interpretation begins with the coefficient, but it does not end with the coefficient.
