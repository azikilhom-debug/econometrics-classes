KEY DISTINCTIONLECTURE 1 · 8/48

The difference between opinion and evidence

OPINION EVIDENCE OPINION — “Advertising increases sales.” This is a logical hypothesis or theoretical view. It requires observations and data to be tested.

EVIDENCE — A systematic analysis of real data on advertising and sales. Evidence can support, reject, or refine an opinion. OPINION ≠ EVIDENCE

THREE PILLARS: 1LECTURE 1 · 15/48

What does economics give us?

Economic theory Simple example Economic theory explains how phenomena may be related and creates theoretical propositions that should be tested.

Simple example — Other things equal, when a product’s price rises, demand may fall. This is a testable economic hypothesis. Economics guides us on what type of relationship we should expect.

THREE PILLARS: 2LECTURE 1 · 16/48

What does mathematics give us?

Demand = f(Price)
This notation says that quantity demanded may be related to price.
Why is it needed?
Why do we need it? Mathematics helps express an economic idea in a short, precise and structured form. At this point we are not calculating anything; we are only defining the relationship.
We introduce the formula after the concept: meaning first, notation second.

Simple fikr Econometric question Simple statement — “Advertising increases sales.” This gives a direction but not a magnitude.

Econometric question — “If advertising expenditure rises by 1 million UZS, by how much does sales change on average?” Econometrics aims not only to identify whether a relationship exists, but also, where possible, to estimate its magnitude.
